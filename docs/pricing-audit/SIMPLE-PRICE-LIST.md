## The three columns

| Column | Meaning | Do you type here? |
|---|---|---|
| A — Service | What the service is (installation, servicing, wash, repair, materials, AMC, discounts) | No |
| B — Current Price (RM) | What your website shows today | No |
| C — New Price (RM) | The price you want | **Yes — this is the only column you touch** |

72 rows, one page, grouped in this order: installation → basic servicing → chemical wash → overhaul → gas → repair → dismantle → materials → annual contract → discounts.

Rows ending in **(please confirm)** are prices your website currently prints but that were not in your main price file (`config/site/pricing.ts`). They are shown so nothing is hidden — confirm them or tell me they are wrong.

## What happens after you type a new price

You type it in column C and tell me in chat (e.g. "chemical wash 1.0–1.5 HP jadi 130").
I then: update `config/site/pricing.ts` once, regenerate every page/blog/FAQ/calculator/schema that quotes it, open a PR, and show you the change before it goes live.

Nothing on the website changes until I make that commit. The sheet itself is not connected to the site — there is no automatic trigger. That is deliberate: a typo in a live sheet must never silently change a price on your website.
